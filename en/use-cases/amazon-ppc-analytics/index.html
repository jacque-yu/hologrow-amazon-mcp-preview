FAQ

Amazon PPC analytics: questions from real operating decisions

What should an Amazon PPC audit include?

Campaign spend, compatible attributed sales, attribution maturity, search-term relevance and product margin or stock constraints. Separate data gaps from performance problems before proposing bid or budget changes.

What is the difference between ACoS and TACoS?

ACoS uses advertising-attributed sales as its denominator; TACoS uses total sales for a compatible scope. Their windows and definitions need to be stated. Neither alone proves incremental profit.

How do I find wasted Amazon ad spend?

Review mature conversion evidence with a minimum observation threshold, query relevance and margin context. A term with no reported orders today may still convert within the attribution window, so zero orders is a review signal rather than automatic waste.

Can Amazon PPC software factor in FBA inventory?

This analysis can compare promoted ASINs with available stock and supplied replenishment plans. A low-stock product may constrain scaling even with strong ROAS. The result is a budget-review recommendation, not an automatic campaign pause.

Can I calculate search-term ACoS from every report?

Only if spend and compatible attributed sales are available at that term grain. The public Hologrow search-term preview does not document all conversion fields. Verify the workspace or provide an authorized conversion export first.

Will this optimize Amazon bids automatically?

No. Hologrow prepares data and the AI workflow creates a review queue. Bids, budgets and negative keywords remain actions for an authorized operator or separately configured advertising tool.
