Cross-functional what-if scenario planning

Sales wants a promotion. Can supply and cash keep up?

What-if analysis changes selected assumptions while holding a documented baseline constant. A proposed Hologrow-supported model combines commerce history with supplied demand responses, lead times and payment terms to compare business scenarios; it does not infer guaranteed causal effects.

Security · data access and sharing

Keep control of what you connect, analyze and share.

Before using Cross-functional what-if scenario planning, agree on the accounts, inputs and people involved in the analysis.

Start with the authorized scope

Confirm the accounts and marketplaces needed for baseline and alternatives. Use the connected-data section to identify required reports; verify available history and permissions in your workspace.

Analysis does not authorize account changes

Scenario model composed with Hologrow inputs and an external calculation workflow. Inputs and equations must be validated; recommendations do not authorize procurement, promotion or payment actions.

Review sensitive details before sharing

Before sending an answer to an AI client or team channel, check whether it needs customer identifiers, unit costs or bank details. Share only the necessary fields and confirm who can access the destination.

Give external tools their own boundaries

Slack delivery, CMS publishing and other write actions require separately configured tools and authorization. Confirm recipients, approval steps and the tool’s data-handling settings before enabling a workflow.

FAQ

Cross-functional what-if scenario planning: questions from real operating decisions

What is cross-functional what-if analysis?

It changes explicit assumptions in a shared baseline and compares effects across sales, supply and finance. The result is a conditional scenario, not a guaranteed forecast or an automatic business action.

Can I model a promotion’s impact on profit and inventory together?

Yes, with price, assumed demand response, costs, sellable stock and receipt dates. Compare multiple demand cases and identify constraints rather than presenting one assumed uplift as the most likely outcome.

Where do demand-elasticity assumptions come from?

They must be supplied or estimated through an appropriate analysis with its uncertainty stated. Historical price and sales co-movement alone does not establish causal elasticity because promotions, availability and seasonality may also change.

How does scenario planning differ from cash forecasting?

Scenario planning compares decisions and their trade-offs across functions. Cash forecasting schedules receipts and payments under assumptions. A cash model can be one component of a broader sales, margin and supply scenario.

Can I compare supplier terms and advertising budgets in one model?

Yes, if both use the same products, periods, currency and baseline assumptions. Show each changed input separately so the team can tell which choice creates the profit or cash effect.

Will the chosen scenario update budgets or purchase orders?

No. Hologrow data plus external AI or modeling tools prepares the comparison. Approved execution requires separate authorized systems and owners; the scenario itself commits nothing.
