FAQ

Ecommerce data integration: questions from real operating decisions

How do I combine Amazon and Shopify data for ecommerce analytics?

Align channel orders through approved product mappings and consistent currency, timezone and revenue definitions. Preserve source totals and show adjustments for discounts, refunds, tax and shipping rather than forcing totals to match.

Why do Shopify and Amazon sales dashboards disagree?

They may use different order statuses, date boundaries or treatments of tax and refunds. Compare the same business event first. A definitional difference can look like a growth difference until normalized.

Can I build a cross-platform product profitability report?

Yes, when product mapping, costs and channel-specific deductions are available. Keep shared-spend allocation explicit and unknown costs separate. Comparable sales alone is not enough to establish comparable profit.

Can Hologrow data feed Looker Studio or Google Sheets?

A configured export, warehouse or external workflow can prepare data for your chosen destination. Verify the actual connection path and refresh process. This is a composed workflow, not a promise that every BI tool has a native connector.

Should advertising-attributed revenue be added to ecommerce sales?

No. Advertising systems may attribute commerce orders already counted in the store. Show attribution beside order revenue, with windows and overlap caveats, rather than summing them into a larger sales total.

Does cross-platform integration merge customer identities automatically?

No. This scenario focuses on business measures at appropriate product, channel and date grains. Person-level identity resolution requires separate lawful data, permissions and a verified method; it is not inferred from matching names or emails.
