FAQ

Automated WBR and MBR reporting: scope, setup and limitations

How are a WBR and an MBR different?

A WBR prioritizes near-term operating exceptions and follow-ups. An MBR reviews broader performance against plans, mix changes and resource decisions.

Can reports be automatically posted to Slack, Lark or email?

Only after the chosen destination, permissions, recipients and delivery behavior are configured and verified. This page does not imply native support for every channel.

What happens when source data arrives late?

The report should show its cutoff and omitted sources. A revised report retains a separate version and explains changed conclusions.

Is this the same as agency reporting?

This page owns the recurring review and delivery workflow. Agency solutions address client isolation, permissions and account-specific service operations.

Can I automate a weekly sales report to Slack with n8n or Zapier?

Yes, as a composed workflow after the data retrieval step, scheduler and Slack destination are configured. Verify channel access, report checks and delivery logging; this is not a claim of a native Hologrow Slack app.
