FAQ

Automated WBR and MBR reporting: questions from real operating decisions

What is the difference between WBR and MBR reporting?

A weekly business review focuses on recent exceptions and operating actions. A monthly business review explains broader drivers, plan variance and resource decisions. They can share definitions while using different time horizons and narratives.

Can I automate a weekly business report to Slack?

Yes, as a combined workflow: Hologrow prepares available data, AI drafts the report, and configured n8n or Zapier automation handles approved Slack delivery. Channel permissions, source readiness and failure handling must be configured.

Which KPIs belong in an ecommerce WBR?

Use a small set tied to decisions, such as comparable revenue, defined contribution, advertising spend and stock risks. Include the prior period, target where supplied, source dates and an owner for material exceptions.

What happens when data is late before the review?

Use a readiness rule: delay a critical report or label a partial pack under the agreed policy. Identify the missing source and its effect on conclusions. Do not fill gaps with fabricated estimates just to meet a schedule.

How can n8n or Zapier avoid duplicate business reports?

Use a stable period-and-version identifier, track the destination and retain delivery receipts. Before retrying, check whether the prior run already sent a message. A successful AI draft is not proof of successful publication.

Can automated reports assign action owners?

The draft can propose owners from an approved responsibility map and include existing due dates. Actual task assignment or messaging requires the configured external tool and authorization. Keep proposed actions distinct from already assigned work.
