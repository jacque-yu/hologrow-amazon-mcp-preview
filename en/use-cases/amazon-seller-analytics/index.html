FAQ

Amazon Seller analytics: profit, PPC, stock and payouts.

How does Amazon Seller analytics differ from Seller Central Business Reports?

Seller Central Business Reports cover sales and traffic. This Hologrow use case prepares available ads, FBA inventory, settlements and authorized cost data so an AI client can compare sales, spending and stock within the same marketplace, product and period. It adds cross-source analysis; exact tables and historical coverage depend on your connected accounts.

How do I analyze ASIN profitability when Amazon sales grow but profit falls?

Agree a contribution-profit definition, then deduct applicable refunds, discounts, platform/FBA fees, ads and landed cost from product sales. Compare the same ASINs and periods, apply costs by effective date and avoid deducting discounts twice. Keep products with missing costs in a separate review list rather than assuming zero cost and reporting them as profitable.

What is the difference between ACoS and TACoS in Amazon PPC analysis?

ACoS divides ad spend by advertising-attributed sales within a consistent attribution window. TACoS divides ad spend by total sales for a compatible scope. A lower ACoS does not necessarily mean higher store profit; review fees, costs, refunds and stock as well. Do not add attributed sales to store sales, and account for recent conversion backfill.

How can FBA inventory data identify SKUs at risk of stocking out?

Divide sellable units in the inventory snapshot by average daily demand over fourteen or twenty-eight complete days, then compare cover with expected usable receipt dates and safety stock. Reserved, unsellable and inbound units are not immediately available stock. Treat launches, zero-demand SKUs and promotions separately. Without a replenishment ETA, flag low cover without claiming a precise supply-gap duration.

Why does my Amazon Settlement Report differ from sales and bank deposits?

Order dates, posted financial events and deposit dates are different. Reconcile one settlement ID, currency and period, separating principal, refunds, fees and adjustments before comparing source settlement totals and bank records. Timing, missing detail and reserves may require investigation; leave unexplained amounts open when the supporting source is unavailable.

What should I check when Amazon Buy Box share declines?

Compare child-ASIN Buy Box share, sessions, conversion and listing status over the same period. If traffic holds up while conversion falls, investigate sellable inventory, price and listing state before deciding how to change advertising. These signals guide an investigation; a change in share alone does not establish why the Buy Box was lost.

Can I automatically send an Amazon Seller WBR to Slack?

A combined workflow can do this: Hologrow prepares Seller business data, AI drafts the WBR under agreed definitions, and a separately configured n8n or Zapier workflow handles scheduling, review and Slack delivery. Configure channel permissions, source readiness and failure handling. This is not a promise of native automatic Slack delivery by Hologrow.

Will connecting Amazon Seller data let Hologrow change ads or listings?

No. Hologrow accesses connected sources read-only and does not modify Seller Central listings, prices, advertising, inventory or orders. Bidding, promotion and replenishment items in an analysis are recommendations for review; authorized people or separately configured tools perform any changes.
