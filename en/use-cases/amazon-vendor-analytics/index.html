FAQ

Amazon Vendor Analytics: scope, setup and limitations

How is Vendor analytics different from Seller analytics?

Vendor analytics follows a 1P wholesale model with purchase orders, receipts, shortages, chargebacks, remittances, and Amazon retail measures. Seller analytics follows 3P consumer orders, fees, settlements, and marketplace inventory.

Can the page combine sell-in and sell-through?

It can compare them, but it should not add them together. Each measure needs an explicit definition and grain.

Does Hologrow approve or dispute chargebacks?

No. Hologrow prepares and analyzes the data for review; operational actions remain with your team and systems.
