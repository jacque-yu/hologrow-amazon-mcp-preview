FAQ

Amazon Vendor Analytics: questions from real operating decisions

How is Amazon Vendor analytics different from Seller analytics?

Vendor is a wholesale relationship with Amazon; Seller operates marketplace sales. Vendor review follows purchase orders, shipments and wholesale payments while using retail reports as demand context. These revenues should not be treated as interchangeable.

Why are Vendor Central retail sales higher than my remittance?

Retail sales reflect Amazon selling to consumers. Remittance reflects payment against your wholesale invoices, after applicable deductions and timing. Explain the difference with invoices and payment evidence, not a direct subtraction of the two totals.

Can I investigate Amazon Vendor shortage claims?

You can assemble supplied deduction records with PO, shipment and receipt evidence. Compare disputed quantities and document gaps. This supports review; it does not determine claim validity or automatically file disputes.

How do I measure Vendor fill rate?

Define whether the measure compares ordered, confirmed, shipped or received quantities and fix the period and denominator. Do not mix stages. Missing receipt evidence prevents a verified received-fill-rate calculation.

Can Vendor forecasting reports inform replenishment?

Yes. Compare the same forecast periods with on-hand inventory and expected receipts. Mean, p70, p80 and p90 forecasts are different demand scenarios, not quantities to add together. Receipt dates are needed for time-phased planning.

Are chargeback and remittance reports included in the public schema?

The public Vendor preview lists operational and retail tables. Do not infer deduction or remittance coverage from that preview. Verify the connected workspace or supply authorized statements before building a payment analysis.
