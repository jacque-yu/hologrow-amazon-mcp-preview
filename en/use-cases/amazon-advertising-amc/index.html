FAQ

Amazon Advertising & AMC: questions from real operating decisions

What is Amazon Marketing Cloud used for in this workflow?

AMC supplies eligible aggregated advertising-path, frequency or audience results. Hologrow business data adds sales, stock and cost context at compatible grain. Query availability and datasets must be verified for the account.

Can AMC and Seller Central data be analyzed together?

Yes, through compatible aggregate product, date and account scopes. Do not join exported person-level identities. Keep advertising conversion definitions separate from retail order totals to avoid double counting.

Does a higher AMC conversion rate prove incremental lift?

No. Different paths or frequency groups can contain different audiences. Observed conversion differences support hypotheses; incremental lift needs an appropriate experimental or causal design.

How should new-to-brand metrics influence media budgets?

Compare eligible new-to-brand measures with their denominator, window and product economics. Check stock before proposing expansion. New-to-brand share is not identical to lifetime value or proof that advertising created the purchase.

Why are some AMC query results missing or suppressed?

Privacy rules or dataset coverage may prevent a group from appearing. Preserve those limitations, check the query metadata and do not reconstruct hidden counts from other rows. Missing groups are not zero conversions.

Does Hologrow run AMC audience activation automatically?

This scenario analyzes authorized aggregate outputs and business context. Audience creation, activation and campaign edits require separate verified tooling and permissions; they are not performed by this demo.
