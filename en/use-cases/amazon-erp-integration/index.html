FAQ

Amazon ERP Integration: questions from real operating decisions

What does Amazon ERP integration mean for analytics?

It means aligning Amazon transactions with authorized ERP products, costs and operational records for analysis. The exact connection method depends on available connectors or supplied files; this page does not promise universal ERP synchronization.

How should Amazon SKUs map to ERP item codes?

Maintain an approved mapping with marketplace, seller SKU, ERP code and effective dates. Represent bundles and pack conversions explicitly. Resolve ambiguous mappings before allocating cost instead of relying on product-name similarity.

Can NetSuite or other ERP costs be used in Amazon profit analysis?

Authorized exports or a verified integration can supply item costs and dates. Confirm field coverage, currencies and update process for your ERP. The workflow applies those inputs; it does not imply a native NetSuite connector is enabled.

How do I handle cost changes during a month?

Use the cost version effective on the relevant business date and preserve the prior version for comparison. Detect overlaps and gaps. Applying the latest cost to all historical sales can rewrite historical margins incorrectly.

Can ERP integration fix bundle profitability?

It can make the calculation auditable when an approved bill of materials, quantities and component costs are available. Add packaging or assembly costs where relevant. Missing components should remain an exception rather than a zero-cost assumption.

Will this workflow write changes back to my ERP?

No. It prepares mapping, reconciliation and cost exceptions for review. Updating ERP masters, posting journals or changing inventory requires a separate authorized process with its own controls.
