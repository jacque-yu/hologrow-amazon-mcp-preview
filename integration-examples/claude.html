BEFORE YOU CONNECT

A few useful answers.

Your sources. Your control.

Review coverage and permissions before you connect. Hologrow reads the source data; your team stays in charge of changes.

Read the product documentation
Which data can I bring into Claude?+

Actively synced sources include Amazon Seller, Amazon Vendor, Amazon Ads, Google Ads, GA4, Google Search Console and Shoplazza. Shopify, Meta Ads, Amazon DSP and Lingxing ERP coverage is available or expanding. Check your account’s Data Schema for the tables actually available.

Does this work with Claude web, Desktop and Code?+

Yes. Claude web and Desktop use the connector authorization flow. Claude Code uses the hosted MCP endpoint with an MCP key. Your Claude plan and workspace permissions must allow the connection; follow the relevant setup guide.

Can Hologrow change my accounts?+

No. Hologrow reads synchronized data. It does not change bids, budgets, listings, inventory or source-account settings. Analysis and recommendations remain for your team to review.

How fresh is the data?+

Hologrow runs scheduled incremental syncs and historical backfills. Report readiness depends on the source, account and table. Check the last successful sync and available date range in Data Explorer before comparing periods.
