Where should we look first?

  1. Start with Amazon.

    Spend increased from $12,000 to $16,800. Review campaign and search-term changes alongside the ASINs driving sales, then check stock availability.

  2. Follow through to the store.

    Google Ads spend rose from $8,000 to $10,000. GA4 sessions rose from 20,000 to 25,000, while store orders fell from 400 to 380. Break down traffic and landing pages to investigate the disconnect; this alone does not establish causation.

  3. Make the decision with the missing context.

    Check refunds, costs, attribution windows and product mappings before claiming profit or reallocating budget. I can prepare the review; Hologrow will not change your campaigns.

04 / WHY A DATA LAYER

A connector opens the door. Context makes it useful.

Official MCP servers can be a good fit for platform-specific work. Hologrow adds a maintained data layer when your business question spans several sources.

01

Several platforms. Several contexts.

An official server exposes its platform. An Amazon + store + Google question still needs multiple sources and shared definitions.

Bring supported sources into one layer.

Query the synchronized commerce, ads and analytics data through Hologrow, while preserving each source’s business context.

02

A response is not a history strategy.

An API or MCP response follows the source’s report windows, quotas and readiness. Historical storage and cross-source freshness still need a plan.

Sync first. Analyze the available history.

Hologrow runs scheduled syncs and backfills available history. Data Explorer shows the actual coverage and last successful sync.

03

Matching column names is not enough.

A sale, a settlement and an attributed conversion describe different events. Currency, dates and SKU mappings affect the comparison.

Make the definitions inspectable.

Documented tables, fields and business keys help Claude choose the right evidence. Validate mappings and ask it to state definitions and gaps.

Capabilities depend on the official server. Amazon Ads MCP includes campaign operations and reporting; Google Analytics MCP is read-only. Neither should be treated as a universal description of all MCPs.

BEFORE YOU CONNECT

A few useful answers.

Your sources. Your control.

Review coverage and permissions before you connect. Hologrow reads the source data; your team stays in charge of changes.

Read the product documentation
How do I connect Amazon to Claude?+

Authorize Amazon Seller in Hologrow Integrations and check its sync in Data Explorer. In Claude web or Desktop, open Settings → Customize → Connectors → Discover, search for hologrow, and authorize Hologrow. Verify the connected platforms before asking about your business.

Do I need to code or host an MCP server?+

No self-hosted server is needed. Claude web and Desktop use the hosted connector and authorization flow. Your workspace must permit connectors; Team or Enterprise may require an Owner to add Hologrow.

Can I use Claude Code too?+

Yes. Claude Code uses the Hologrow hosted MCP endpoint and an MCP key from AI Platforms. Its setup is different from the web and Desktop connector; follow the Claude Code section of the documentation.

Does connecting Seller Central include Amazon Ads?+

Amazon Seller, Vendor and Ads are separate sources. Connect Amazon Ads as well if you need campaign spend, keywords or attributed sales. Availability depends on the authorized account, marketplace and report permissions.

Can Claude analyze Amazon and my online store together?+

Yes, when the relevant sources and periods are available. Shoplazza, Google Ads and GA4 can complement Amazon data. Align currency, business dates and product mappings first. Ad-attributed sales and actual order sales must not be added together.

Are Shopify, Meta Ads and other sources supported?+

Coverage is expanding and differs by source. The documentation lists Amazon Seller, Vendor and Ads, Shoplazza, Google Ads, GA4 and Search Console as actively synced sources. For Shopify, Meta Ads, Amazon DSP and Lingxing ERP, confirm the current tables in your account before planning an analysis.

What is a schema, and do I need to understand it?+

A schema is the data dictionary: which tables exist, which fields they contain and what each row represents. You can ask Claude in plain language. The schema helps you verify whether the required sales, ads or inventory data is available and how metrics should be interpreted.

Why use Hologrow if an official MCP exists?+

An official MCP can be useful for its own platform. Hologrow focuses on a shared, synchronized commerce data layer across supported sources, with documented schemas and visible freshness. Choose based on whether you need a platform operation or a cross-source business analysis.

How fresh is the data? How much history is available?+

Syncs are scheduled, not guaranteed real time. Initial syncs and backfills vary by source and table, and upstream report delays still apply. Use Data Explorer to check the last successful sync and earliest/latest available business dates before comparing periods.

Can Claude change my bids, listings or inventory?+

Hologrow MCP is read-only. It does not post, bid or change source-account settings. Claude can help analyze and prepare recommendations; your team reviews and executes changes in the appropriate tools.

How do I revoke access or disconnect a source?+

Revoke OAuth sessions or rotate MCP keys in AI Platforms to stop AI access. Disconnecting a source in Integrations is a separate action: Hologrow stops syncing that account and immediately deletes its synchronized data.

Can these answers establish profit or justify more ad spend?+

Only when the necessary inputs support the conclusion. Profit also requires costs, fees, refunds and validated mappings. Incomplete periods, missing costs and different attribution windows must be made explicit. The conversation on this page uses synthetic data to demonstrate the workflow.
