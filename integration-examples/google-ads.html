FAQ

Before your first question.

A clear starting point.

Data coverage, setup and control—so you know what to expect when you connect.

Read the documentation
What does “Try Google Ads now” do?+

It helps you choose or write a business question. Enter takes you to Hologrow to connect your account; this page does not run an analysis. Use Copy question to keep your prompt, then paste it into your connected AI client after the data is ready.

Which Google Ads data is available?+

Documented coverage includes customers, campaigns, ad groups, ads and keywords, plus daily performance and search terms where available. Availability depends on the account, permissions and reports. Hourly click and hourly campaign statistics are outside the documented scope.

What do I need to connect?+

A Hologrow account and authorization for the relevant Google Ads account. In Integrations, select Google Ads and follow the connection form, including the Google Ads Customer ID and the required authorization details. Use Data Explorer to confirm synchronization.

Can the AI change bids or pause campaigns?+

Hologrow provides read-only access to synchronized data. It does not change bids, budgets or campaign settings. Your team reviews recommendations and makes changes in the source platform.

Is this live data? How much history is available?+

AI queries Hologrow’s synchronized data layer. Hologrow runs scheduled syncs and backfills available history; freshness and date coverage vary by source and table. Check the latest successful sync and available dates in Data Explorer. Retention is not a promise of unlimited or permanent history.

Can I combine Google Ads with Amazon or my store?+

Connect the relevant supported sources, such as Amazon, Shoplazza and GA4. Compare aligned dates, currencies and business keys. Google Ads conversion value, GA4 events and store orders use different definitions; parallel changes alone do not establish attribution or causation.

Which AI clients can I use?+

Hologrow documents connections for Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor and other supported clients. Follow the guide for your client and workspace. Hologrow prepares the data; your chosen AI performs the analysis.

Why might CPA or ROAS differ from my dashboard?+

Check account currency, time zone, dates, conversion definitions, attribution windows and data freshness. The cost_micros field must be divided by 1,000,000 to obtain cost in the account currency. Late conversions may revise recent periods, and ratios should use matching totals.

How can I stop access?+

You can revoke AI authorization in AI Platforms. Disconnecting a source is a separate action: the documentation states that its synchronized data is deleted immediately. Check the impact before disconnecting a source you still use.
